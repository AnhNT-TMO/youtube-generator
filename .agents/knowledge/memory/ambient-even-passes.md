For relaxing Christmas background tracks, the three melody passes stay close in loudness and texture.
- Pass 1 should not start low.
- Pass 3 is only a little higher than pass 2. It should not stand out as a climax: no new lead instrument taking over the melody, and no big organ swell.
- Avoid long, very quiet fade-in intros.

**Why:** the music is atmosphere. Big changes pull the listener's attention and break the relaxed mood.

**How to apply:**
- Keep the section RMS spread across passes within about 1.5 dB, with roughly +0.5 dB per pass.
- Build by small additions (one soft layer, slightly denser support), not by swapping the lead or making sudden texture jumps.
- Measure per-section RMS before delivery.
