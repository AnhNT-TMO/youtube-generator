CEO 2026-10-06: "kênh của tôi là piano". In every ambient vibe (snowglobe, church, and any new one such as jazz, lofi, fireside, celtic, lullaby), piano plays the carol melody all the way through. Other instruments (harp, kalimba, strings, organ, choir, drums, ambience) are only background support, never the lead.

**Why:** the channel identity is piano; a vibe led by another instrument stops being the channel's sound (flute-lead orchestral was rejected 10-05 for the same reason).

**How to apply:** only research/propose vibes whose genre is already piano-led (e.g. piano jazz café, piano lofi, piano + fireplace/nature ambience). Genres led by another instrument (Celtic harp, music box/kalimba lullaby, sax jazz, acoustic guitar) are simply skipped — do not convert them to piano (CEO clarified 10-06).
