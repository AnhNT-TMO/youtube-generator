CEO 2026-10-06 agreed:
- Each Christmas ambient album uses exactly one vibe (church, snowglobe, or a new piano-led one). The album is about 3 hours, so 30–40 distinct carols repeat in a new order.
- Only the carols that fit that vibe are picked. Do not render every carol in every vibe. For example, lofi takes only 4/4 or 2/4 carols, and jazz takes the bouncy secular ones.
- The PM drafts the fit table in `research/christmas-carols/VIBE_FIT.md`.

**Why:**
- The title and thumbnail promise one atmosphere.
- Background listeners leave when the sound jumps.
- Variety between albums lowers the risk of YouTube treating reused music as reused content.

**How to apply:**
- Plan each album as vibe → fitting carols, and render only those.
- Add ambience such as fire or rain as one continuous layer at assembly, not baked into each track.
