The user is a software developer and says they cannot judge music quality by ear (tried picking Album 001 tracks and didn't trust their own choices). They want to "industrialize" album production: selection and checks should be driven by measurable criteria (tempo, vocal similarity, genre fit, defects) rather than asking them "which sounds better".

**How to apply:** when a music decision comes up, lead with numbers from the verification-audio skill (`verify.py`, `album_check.py`) and say plainly what the numbers can and can't tell; only ask the user to listen to a specific flagged section (REVIEW), not for open-ended taste judgments.
