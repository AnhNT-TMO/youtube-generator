CEO 2026-10-09: Mureka can't be forced to keep the exact note sequence, so an "inspired" arrangement is accepted. Hard line: the listener must still recognize the carol, not hear a different song. Deck the Halls and Silent Night test takes pass, because the first ~1 minute is piano-led and the melody is easy to hear there.

**Why:** fidelity measurements showed Mureka keeps key and harmony but not the note order (sequence ≈ shuffled in most takes), and prompting for thin accompaniment doesn't change that.

**How to apply:** judge takes on the opening (pass 1), not whole-song fidelity. Pass 1 pitch-class should be clearly above control: on 10-09 every take was +11 to +37 points. Takes whose opening isn't piano-led or loses the melody are flagged for the CEO to listen to (see [[mureka-flag-not-reject]]).
