For video motion (and similar execution details), the CEO approves the PM's plan described in text; after that the PM judges the rendered result (loop, qa, full video) and ships it without sending previews back for CEO approval (CEO 2026-09-29, album 002: "không cần tôi duyệt đâu, PM thấy được là được, tôi đã duyệt bản text rồi").

**Why:** the CEO wants to decide direction, not re-check execution; waiting on CEO previews slows every album.

**How to apply:** present motion/effect plans per album in words (per axis vs the previous album) → once the CEO OKs the text, render, QC (qa PASS + look at frames), package and report the result. Pictures (thumbnail + Short image) still go to the CEO for review before production, as in [[pm-owns-length-and-pictures]] and [[company-model]].
