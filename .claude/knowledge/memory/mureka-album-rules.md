CEO 2026-10-09 approved the Mureka album plan. One loop of 20–22 songs plays three times in the same order. Neighbouring songs change bed. No choir (CEO dropped it the same day, see [[mureka-voice-accent]]). Keys follow the ambient key standard, including the seam back to track 1. Tier I songs open the loop. Christmas albums have no YouTube chapters. The numbers live in research/christmas-carols/MUREKA-ALBUM.json.

The CEO heard drums in silent_night_strings_glockenspiel_v2, and Demucs confirmed it: the drums stem carries 4.4 % of the energy. mureka_pick.py flags takes with percussion; the CEO listens and decides, see [[mureka-flag-not-reject]].

**Why:** a 3 h background album needs variety (beds) without forcing reshuffles. Mureka sometimes adds percussion the prompt never asked for.

**How to apply:** don't build the album tool or start batch generation until the CEO asks; album 006 (loop.json) is the first planned loop. When an album is planned, plan the loop first and then generate only the planned style per song. Related: [[mureka-recognizable-opening]], [[mureka-voice-accent]], [[christmas-ambient-standard]].
