2026-09-25: R&D report on open-source singing/lip-sync models (InfiniteTalk, LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.5, LiveAvatar, Wan2.2-S2V;
max 720p, ~1–2.5 GPU-h for 2 Shorts × 50 s, singing quality unverified) → CEO decided: not worth it, keep still images + loop
effects for albums, singles and Shorts. Future: research **Gemini (Veo)** video generation instead of open-source models.

**Why:** cost per video too high for the gain; open-source quality on singing unproven.

**How to apply:** don't propose installing lip-sync models; when moving pictures come up again, start from Gemini/Veo research
(PM proposal to the CEO). Related: [[company-model]].
